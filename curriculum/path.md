# Learning path

Data: [`letters.json`](letters.json) — 28 letters in 7 islands, each letter with its sound, positional forms (generated in the app), 3 example words with emoji, English/French translations, and **home words** (common dialect forms) where the standard word differs from what many kids hear at home.

## Levels
| Level | Name | Goal | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Letter islands** | Recognise all 28 letters by shape and sound, in all 4 positions | Building now (MVP) |
| 1 | **Little vowels** | Fatha, kasra, damma, sukun; read syllables (بَ بِ بُ) | Next |
| 2 | **First words** | Decode 2–3 letter words; long vowels ا و ي; shadda; ة | Planned |
| 3 | **Little stories** | Read 3–5 sentence illustrated stories, with home-dialect bridge on every page | Planned |

## Why this letter order (not alphabetical)
1. **Palm Island — ا ب ت ث:** one shape, different dots. Kids learn "dots change the letter" on day one.
2. **Moon Bay — م ن ي ل:** very frequent letters with clear sounds.
3. **Desert of Lonely Letters — ر ز د ذ:** introduces the idea that 6 letters (ا د ذ ر ز و) never connect to the next one.
4. **Sun Mountain — س ش و ه:** teeth shapes and the shape-shifting ه.
5. **Camel Valley — ج ح خ ع:** the throat sounds; heritage kids often *hear* these well, which is a confidence boost.
6. **Garden of Dots — ف ق ك غ.**
7. **Castle of Strong Letters — ص ض ط ظ:** emphatic sounds, contrasted with س د ت ذ learned earlier.

## Lesson shape (≈8 minutes)
1. **Meet** the letter: big letter, name, sound, 3 picture words (≈1 min)
2. **Forms**: the 4 positional shapes with a "where in the word" animation (≈1 min)
3. **Play** 3 short games mixing the new letter with earlier ones (≈5 min)
4. **Reward + family moment** (≈1 min)

## Dialect bridge rule
We teach **standard spelling** for reading, and show the **home word** next to it when they differ (e.g. طَمَاطِم ↔ بَنَدُورَة / قُوطَة / مْطِيشَة). Message to the child: *"Both are Arabic! At home we say one, in books we write the other."*
