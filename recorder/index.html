How to record (read once)
  1. Quiet room, phone about 20 cm from your mouth. Soft furnishings help (a closet full of clothes is perfect).
  2. Speak clear standard Arabic (فصحى), warm and friendly, a little slower than normal — like reading to a child.
  3. Press Record, wait half a second, say the text once, then press Stop. Listen back; re-record if needed.
  4. "Sound" clips are just the short sound (بَ), not the letter name.
  5. Same person, same place and same distance for every clip.
  6. When finished, press Download all and send the .zip file.
