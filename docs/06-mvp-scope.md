# 06 · MVP scope

Goal: a **free, installable web app** that a 5–8 year old can use alone for 10 minutes a day to learn all 28 letters, used by 20–50 pilot families.

## In
- **Alphabet path:** 28 letters, in order of teachability (not alphabetical), grouped in 7 "islands" of 4 letters.
- **Per letter:** name + sound with audio, the 4 positional forms (isolated / initial / medial / final), 3 example words with emoji illustration, short-vowel sounds (fatha, kasra, damma).
- **Games (4):**
  1. *Listen & pick* — hear a letter, tap it.
  2. *Shape match* — match a letter to its connected form.
  3. *First sound* — see a word picture, choose the first letter.
  4. *Trace* — trace the letter with a finger.
- **Rewards:** stars per game, stickers per island, daily streak.
- **Profiles:** up to 4 children on one device (stored on device).
- **Parent corner:** progress per child, weekly tip, "family moment" for each island. Behind a simple parent gate.
- **Interface languages:** English and French.
- **Offline:** works without internet after first load (PWA).

## Out (for now)
Accounts and cloud sync, payments, teacher dashboard, stories, speech recognition, app stores, dialect selection beyond example labels.

## Audio approach
v0 uses the device's built-in Arabic speech voice where available (fast to ship, uneven quality). Before the pilot we replace it with **recorded audio from a native speaker** — the single biggest quality lever for this audience.

## Success criteria for the pilot
- 60% of children complete island 1 in their first week.
- Week-4 retention ≥ 40%.
- Parents rate "my child enjoys it" ≥ 4/5.
