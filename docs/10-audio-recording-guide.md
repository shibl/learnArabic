# 10 · Audio recording guide

**Recording page:** https://shibl.github.io/learnArabic/recorder/
**Clips:** 196, about 45–60 minutes of recording in total. It can be done over several sittings: progress is saved in the browser.

## What gets recorded
| Type | Count | Example | How to say it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Letter name | 28 | بَاء | The name of the letter, clearly |
| Letter sound | 28 | بَ | Only the short sound with fatha, not the name |
| Syllables | 56 | بِ · بُ | Short sound with kasra, then with damma |
| Words | 84 | بَطَّة | The whole word, fully vowelled as shown |

## Who should record
- One speaker for the whole set: a warm, clear voice, comfortable in **standard Arabic (فصحى)**.
- Any regional accent is fine as long as the standard pronunciation is clear, including the "strong" letters ص ض ط ظ and the throat sounds ح خ ع غ.
- Optional later: a second (male/female) voice for variety.

## Setup (5 minutes)
1. Use a **phone or laptop with Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox**. A wired or Bluetooth headset mic is better than a laptop's built-in mic, but a phone held 20 cm away is fine.
2. Pick the **quietest room** you have. Soft furnishings absorb echo: a bedroom or a closet full of clothes beats a kitchen or bathroom. Turn off fans, AC and notifications.
3. Open the recording page, allow the microphone, and fill in "Speaker details" so the voice can be credited.
4. Do a test: record the first clip, listen back on headphones. You should hear no echo, hum or background noise.

## While recording
- Press **Record**, wait half a second, say the text **once**, press **Stop**. On a computer the space bar also starts and stops.
- It plays back automatically. If it isn't right, just press Record again; it replaces the old take.
- Speak **a little slower than normal**, friendly and smiling, like reading to a 5-year-old. Keep the same energy and distance for every clip.
- For **letter sounds and syllables**, say only the short sound (بَ), not "baa".
- Take a break every 50 clips and drink water; consistency matters more than speed.
- Use **"Jump to next missing"** to find clips you skipped. Green squares in "All clips" are done.

## When finished
1. Press **Download all (.zip)**. You get one file, `sindibad-...-clips.zip`, with every clip plus a list of what each one is.
2. Send me the zip by **attaching it in this chat**. Recordings stay on the device until you download them; nothing is uploaded anywhere automatically.
3. I'll check every clip, trim silence, even out volume, convert to a small format for phones, and switch the app from the robot voice to the real one.

## Consent
By sending the zip, the speaker agrees that Sindibad Studios may use the recordings in Sindibad Letters and its marketing, with credit as given in "Speaker details". A one-line written OK from the speaker is enough.
