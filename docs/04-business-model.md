# 04 · Business model

## Revenue streams
| Stream | Offer | Price (to test) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Family plan** (main) | Full curriculum, up to 4 child profiles, parent reports | **$6.99/mo or $49/yr** (local equivalents in EUR/CAD/GBP) | Below Amal/Lamsa top tiers; annual plan is the target |
| **Free tier** (forever) | Whole alphabet (28 letters) + 1 game per day | $0 | Proves value; parents share it; weekend schools can recommend it without friction |
| **School / community plan** | Teacher dashboard, class codes, homework sets | **$2 per student per month**, billed per term | Weekend schools are where motivated families already gather |
| **Grants & sponsors** (later) | Cultural foundations, embassies' cultural programs | — | Fund free access for low-income families |

## Unit economics (targets)
- Cost to serve a family: < $0.30/month (static web app, audio files on a CDN, no heavy servers).
- Payment fees: ~15–30% on app stores; web checkout preferred where allowed.
- Target customer acquisition cost: < $15 per paying family, mostly through schools, community word of mouth and parent creators.
- Payback: under 4 months on the annual plan.

## Path
1. **Now – Jan 2027:** free web app (PWA), validate with 20–50 pilot families. No payments.
2. **Q1 2027:** add accounts + family plan via web checkout; launch school pilot with 3 weekend schools.
3. **Q2 2027:** wrap the PWA for iOS / Android stores.

## Legal & trust checklist (before collecting any child data)
- Company entity and a privacy policy written for children's apps (COPPA in the US, GDPR-K in the EU, PIPEDA / Quebec Law 25 in Canada).
- Verifiable parental consent before creating accounts; no data on the child beyond a first name / avatar and progress.
- Trademark search for the final brand name.
