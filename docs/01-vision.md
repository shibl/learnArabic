# 01 · Vision & problem

## The problem
Millions of children grow up in Arab families outside the Arab world. At home they hear Arabic — usually a dialect (Levantine, Egyptian, Maghrebi, Gulf, Iraqi…). At school, on screens and with friends, everything is English, French, German, Swedish…

What typically happens:

1. **They understand more than they speak.** Arabic becomes a "listening language" — they answer their parents in the local language.
2. **They can't read or write it.** Reading in Arabic means learning a new script *and* Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى), which differs from what they hear at home. Few apps acknowledge this gap.
3. **Parents feel guilty and stuck.** Weekend schools are far, expensive or once a week. Parents are busy, sometimes rusty readers themselves, and don't know where to start.
4. **Available apps aren't built for them.** Most Arabic apps for kids target either children *in* the Arab world (all-Arabic UI, assume Arabic schooling) or non-Arab adults learning a foreign language.

## The vision
> Every child of an Arab family, wherever they grow up, can read a story in Arabic with their grandparents — and enjoy it.

## What Huroof is
A playful app for children aged **4–10** that teaches them to **read** Arabic and **connect** it to the Arabic they already hear at home, in **5–10 minute daily sessions**, with the **parent in the loop**.

## Principles
- **Heritage-first.** The child already knows sounds and words. We start from what they know (the dialect word for "bread") and link it to the written, standard form (خبز).
- **Interface in the child's school language** (English and French first), content in Arabic. The child is never lost.
- **Short and daily beats long and weekly.** One small win a day, with a streak the family can see.
- **Parents are co-players, not supervisors.** Every lesson ends with a 1-minute "family moment" a parent can do even if their own reading is rusty.
- **Safe by design.** No ads, no chat, no data sold, works offline. Built to COPPA / GDPR-K expectations.
- **Respect the diversity of the Arab world.** Dialect-aware, culturally broad (not one country's view), and neutral on religion — families add what they want.

## What success looks like (first year)
- A child who uses Huroof 5 days a week for 3 months can read all 28 letters in every position and decode simple 3-letter words.
- 40% of families active in week 1 are still active in week 4.
- Parents say they "feel less guilty and more involved."
