# 08 · Go-to-market plan

## Phase 1 — Pilot (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027): 20–50 families
**Goal:** learn, not grow. Find out whether kids come back on their own.

| Channel | Action | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal networks** | The founding team shares with 10–20 Arab families they know | Fast, honest feedback |
| **WhatsApp parent groups** | Short bilingual message + 30-second screen recording | This is where diaspora parents already talk |
| **Weekend Arabic schools** | Email 10 schools in large diaspora cities (e.g. Toronto, Paris, Lyon, Berlin, London): "free homework companion for your students" | Concentrated, motivated families; teachers become recommenders |
| **Mosques / churches / cultural associations** | Flyer with QR code at family events (religion-neutral wording) | Trusted community spaces |

**Pilot measures:** day-7 and day-28 retention, letters completed, a 5-question parent survey, 5 parent interviews.

## Phase 2 — Public launch (Q1–Q2 2027)
- **Parent creators:** partner with 5–10 Arab mum/dad creators on Instagram and TikTok (bilingual family content). Offer free family plans + affiliate.
- **Content:** a weekly short video "One letter in 30 seconds" in EN/FR with the home-dialect twist — shareable, builds the brand, feeds search.
- **SEO:** pages for "teach my child Arabic at home", "apprendre l'arabe à mon enfant", per-letter pages.
- **Ramadan & Eid moments (Feb–Mar 2027):** families are most motivated about heritage; run a "30 days, 28 letters" family challenge.
- **School plan launch** with the pilot schools as references.

## Messaging
- **Core line:** *Your child hears Arabic every day. Now they can read it.*
- **Parent pain:** "They answer me in English." → hope, not guilt.
- **Proof points:** built on home Arabic, 10 minutes a day, no ads, parents in the loop.
- **Tone:** warm, never preachy, inclusive of all Arab countries and all faiths.

## Budget (pilot)
~$0 cash: organic channels only. Paid acquisition is tested only after day-28 retention ≥ 40%.
