## Brand-name shortlist (to replace "Huroof")
"Huroof / حروف" is used by many small apps. Options that keep the heritage-bridge idea and work in EN/FR/AR:

| Name | Arabic | Meaning / why | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hudhud** | هدهد | The hoopoe, our guide character; messenger bird across the Arab world | Memorable, mascot = brand. Check store conflicts |
| **Jisr Kids** | جسر | "Bridge" — home Arabic ↔ written Arabic | Clear story; "Jisr" hard to say for non-Arabs |
| **Bayt Huroof** | بيت الحروف | "House of letters" — Arabic at home | Warm; distinct from plain "Huroof" |
| **Nuqta** | نقطة | "Dot" — the dots that change letters (island 1 lesson) | Short, playful, used by some brands |

Recommendation: **Hudhud** — strongest identity, ties name, mascot and story together. Final pick is the owner's, followed by a trademark and store search.
